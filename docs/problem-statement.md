# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

Rapid KL On-Demand users, especially passengers who frequently use the application to book on-demand transportation.

## What exactly is going wrong?

The application has cluttered map displays, confusing pickup and drop-off location selections, unclear route visualisation, and inaccurate estimated arrival times (ETA).

## Why does it matter?

These issues make it difficult for users to identify suitable locations, understand their journey routes, and plan their travel time. This may lead to confusion, inconvenience, and a poor user experience.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Rapid KL On-Demand users experience difficulties when using the application due to cluttered map displays, confusing pickup and drop-off location selections, unclear route visualisation, and inaccurate estimated arrival times. These issues make it challenging for users to navigate the application and understand their journey details. As a result, users may experience confusion, inconvenience, and difficulties planning their journeys. Therefore, improvements to the application's UI/UX are needed to provide a clearer, simpler, and more user-friendly booking experience.
