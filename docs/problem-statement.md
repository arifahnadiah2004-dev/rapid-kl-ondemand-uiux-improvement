# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

Rapid KL On-Demand users, especially passengers who frequently use the application to book on-demand transportation.

## What exactly is going wrong?

The application has cluttered map displays, confusing pickup and drop-off location selections, unclear route visualisation, and inaccurate estimated arrival times (ETA).

## Why does it matter?

These issues make it difficult for users to identify suitable locations, understand their journey routes, and plan their travel time. This may lead to confusion, inconvenience, and a poor user experience.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Rapid KL On-Demand users experience difficulties when booking and managing their journeys due to cluttered map displays, confusing pickup and drop-off location selections, unclear route visualisation and inaccurate estimated arrival times. The existing interface makes it challenging for passengers to identify suitable locations and understand their actual journey routes. Additionally, differences between the estimated and actual arrival times may cause inconvenience and difficulties in planning their journeys. Therefore, improvements to the application's UI/UX are needed to provide clearer map visualisation, smarter pickup recommendations and a simpler, more user-friendly booking experience.
